To upgrade, run:
    pipx upgrade pybirdsreynolds --include-deps
To install, run:
    pipx install pybirdsreynolds
To force reinstall, run:
    pipx install pybirdsreynolds --force
To uninstall, run:
    pipx uninstall pybirdsreynolds
To force uninstall (if needed), run:
    pipx uninstall pybirdsreynolds --force
